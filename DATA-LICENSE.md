# Data attribution

The generated `resources/data.php` file is derived from **FPS BOSA BeST Address**
open data, published from the authentic regional address registers for Brussels,
Flanders and Wallonia.

Source: <https://opendata.bosa.be/>

The source data is published under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)**.
The MIT license in `LICENSE.md` applies to this package's source code, not to third-party
source data under its own license.
